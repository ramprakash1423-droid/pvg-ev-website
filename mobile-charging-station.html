Product Overview

What is a Mobile Charging Station?

A Mobile Charging Station is an EV-charging system integrated into a commercial vehicle. It can be deployed to customer, fleet or commercial locations to provide charging support without requiring the EV to travel to a conventional fixed charging station.

The solution is designed to provide planned, on-demand and operational charging support at locations where permanent charging infrastructure may be unavailable, insufficient or impractical.

How Mobile Charging Works

Charging workflow diagram

  1. 01Requirement submitted

    Customer shares vehicle, location and charging need.

  2. 02Vehicle and location reviewed

    PVG-EV checks the use case and operating context.

  3. 03Site access checked

    Entry, parking and safe charging space are assessed.

  4. 04Charging need evaluated

    Battery condition, timing and compatibility are reviewed.

  5. 05Pilot or service plan created

    Feasible requests move into controlled planning.

  6. 06Deployment scheduled

    Approved requirements receive a planned support window.

  7. 07Mobile unit dispatched

    The vehicle-mounted charging unit is sent when approved.

  8. 08Charging support delivered

    EV connection and charging are handled under safe procedure.

  9. 09Review and follow-up

    Session information supports pilot learning and readiness.

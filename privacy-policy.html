Privacy Policy

PVG-EV privacy and data-protection wording for website enquiries and pilot registrations.

Data collection

PVG-EV may collect information submitted through contact, fleet-assessment, charging-request and pilot-registration forms so the team can review and respond to the enquiry.

Use of information

Submitted information is used for enquiry handling, pilot-suitability assessment and related service communication. PVG-EV does not use the website forms to sell personal information.

Your choices

You may ask PVG-EV to correct or delete information submitted through this website by using the contact page. Please include enough detail for the team to identify the relevant enquiry.

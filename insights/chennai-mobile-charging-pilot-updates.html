Chennai Mobile Charging Pilot Updates

What the pilot is intended to assess before wider service expansion.

Author: PVG-EV Editorial Team · Publication date: July 12, 2026

Overview

The proposed Chennai pilot is intended to test whether selected mobile-charging use cases can be planned safely and practically. Registration helps identify genuine demand; it is not a service confirmation or a promise of citywide coverage.

What to prepare

  • Describe the vehicle, fleet, community or commercial use case.
  • Share vehicle types, approximate numbers and usual parking periods.
  • Identify the proposed location, access limits and responsible site contact.
  • Explain the charging gap and the outcome the pilot should evaluate.

Current pilot context

PVG-EV is currently defining use cases, partner roles and assessment criteria for a proposed Chennai pilot using Setrans technology. Participation, timing and any deployment would remain subject to screening, testing, approvals and operational readiness.

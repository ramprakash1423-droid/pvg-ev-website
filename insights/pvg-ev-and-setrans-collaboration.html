The PVG-EV and Setrans Collaboration

How technology development and local operating capability combine in the PVG-EV model.

Author: PVG-EV Editorial Team · Publication date: July 12, 2026

Overview

The collaboration separates complementary responsibilities: Setrans develops and engineers the Mobile Charging Station technology, while PVG-EV focuses on local market development, requirement assessment and preparation for potential operations in Tamil Nadu.

What a clear partnership model needs

  • A defined customer problem and a suitable pilot use case.
  • Clear ownership of engineering, local operations and customer communication.
  • Agreed testing, approval, safety and documentation responsibilities.
  • Pilot measures that can inform a decision without overstating readiness.
  • A support and escalation process for every participating organisation.

Current collaboration context

The parties are preparing the Chennai pilot model; this does not establish live service availability or a completed regional rollout. Specific activities would remain subject to agreed scope, technical review, approvals and operating readiness.

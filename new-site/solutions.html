BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

A few things
to know.

Which solution should I choose?+

Choose EV owners for your own vehicle, fleet enquiries for multiple vehicles or depot operations, and property enquiries for apartments or workplaces. For a short-term event or project, start with the event section. If you’re unsure, talk to our team.

Can I enquire about a location outside Chennai?+

Yes. Share your location and requirement with the team. The first focus is the Chennai pilot; enquiries from other locations help inform future planning and do not confirm coverage.

What if I’m away from the charging site?+

The charging form requests GPS automatically at its Location step, with your browser’s permission. Complete it while you are at the charging site, or contact the team to discuss a different location.

Does an enquiry confirm a charging visit?+

An enquiry starts a conversation. The team reviews vehicle compatibility, site suitability, availability and pricing before agreeing a visit. The form does not provide emergency dispatch.

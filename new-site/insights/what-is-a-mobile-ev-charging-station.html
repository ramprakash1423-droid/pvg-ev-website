What Is a Mobile EV Charging Station?

A practical explanation of vehicle-mounted charging systems and how they support EV users.

THE TAKEAWAY

Start with the vehicle, the place and the time available. These details help determine whether mobile charging is a suitable option.

A mobile EV charging station carries charging equipment to a vehicle or operating location instead of requiring every EV to travel to a fixed charger. The model is intended for planned, temporary or location-specific needs where access and compatibility can be reviewed in advance.

What to check before planning

  • Vehicle type, connector information and the charging need.
  • Exact location, entry route, parking space and safe working area.
  • Preferred timing and how long the vehicle can remain stationary.
  • Whether fixed charging is unavailable, insufficient or still being planned.
  • Who can approve access and coordinate the vehicle on site.

PVG-EV pilot context

PVG-EV is preparing a Chennai pilot around Setrans Mobile Charging Station technology. Technical values, service coverage, timing and deployment remain subject to review, testing, approvals and operating readiness.

Cookies & browser storage

What this website stores in your browser.

This version of the website

The website code does not set advertising, analytics or preference cookies. Enquiry drafts use session storage in your current browser tab so you can reload or move between steps without losing your entries. Drafts can include contact, vehicle and location details you enter. They are cleared after a successful submission or when you choose “Start again”. Closing the tab normally ends this storage; browser session-restoration features may restore it. Consent must be selected again after a reload.

Previous visits

An older version may have saved a cookie-notice preference in local storage or a charging-enquiry draft in session storage. You can clear old website data using your browser’s site-data settings.

Location permission

Your browser asks before sharing your location. GPS is optional; an address or maps link can be entered instead. Location permission is controlled by your browser, not a website cookie.

Other services

The site host may process technical request information. When you choose an external service such as WhatsApp, that service’s own storage and privacy practices apply. Enquiry storage is explained in the privacy notice.

Managing site data

Use your browser settings to review permissions or clear existing cookies and stored website data. Contact PVG-EV if you have questions about an enquiry.

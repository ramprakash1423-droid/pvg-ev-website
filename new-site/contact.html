A LITTLE CLARITY

Before you
say hello.

A few useful things to know before starting a conversation.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

The team reviews your message and contact details, then follows up about the requirement. For charging enquiries, availability, compatibility, timing and pricing must be confirmed before a visit.

Do I need to know all the technical details?

No. Start with what you know. Your vehicle model, location or a short description of your idea can help the team understand the next step.

Can I enquire from outside Chennai?

Yes. Chennai is the current pilot focus, and other locations can register interest for future planning. Enquiring does not confirm service coverage in your area.

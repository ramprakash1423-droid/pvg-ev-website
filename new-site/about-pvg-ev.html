WHY WE’RE HERE

Life keeps moving.
Charging should
find its place.

A vehicle on its daily route. A resident’s parking space. A workplace preparing for EVs. Each starts with a different charging need.

Our purpose is to understand those needs and explore where mobile charging can complement existing infrastructure. We start with how a vehicle is used, where it can park and the time available to charge.

That means working with owners, operators and site hosts to shape a practical requirement before proposing a solution.

Common questions

A little clarity before you enquire.

Is charging available everywhere?

No. Chennai is the pilot focus. Every location and date needs confirmation.

Will this work with my EV?

Share your make, model and connector if known. Compatibility is reviewed before any service is agreed.

Can I request emergency roadside dispatch?

This website does not provide live emergency dispatch. If you are stranded or unsafe, contact your vehicle’s roadside assistance provider or local emergency services as appropriate.

How much does charging cost?

Request a review of your requirement. Pricing and payment terms must be agreed before service.

Fleet planning worksheet

Use this checklist to prepare a clear enquiry. It does not confirm service availability or replace a technical site assessment.

  1. Fleet profile

    Record vehicle models, connector types and the number of vehicles per shift.

  2. Daily pattern

    Record departure charge, route distance, return time and overnight dwell time.

  3. Current charging

    List chargers used, travel time, queues and recurring charging gaps.

  4. Pilot scope

    Identify a small vehicle group, location and period for review.

  5. Success measures

    Agree readiness, charging time and cost measures before any pilot.

Before you proceed

Confirm suitability, availability, commercial terms and responsibilities directly with the team.

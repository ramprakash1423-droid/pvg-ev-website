Privacy notice

How enquiry information is used.

Who handles your enquiry

PVG-EV is an initiative of Prime Ventures Global. To ask about information provided through this website, call +91 97510 83000 or use the contact form.

What you choose to share

Forms collect your name, mobile number and the details relevant to your enquiry. Depending on the form, this can include your email, company, vehicle, city, charging address, timing and message. The Charging Request form automatically requests your GPS location when you reach the Location step. Your browser must permit location access before coordinates can be captured. You can retry or clear the captured location, and no continuous location tracking is performed. The Charging Request form requires GPS and an exact location. If you cannot share GPS, you can use the Contact form or call the team instead.

How it is used

The information is used to review your requirement, contact you and coordinate a possible service or partnership. Launch updates are requested separately through the pilot form. You can ask the team to stop updates.

Services involved

Submitted enquiries are sent to the website’s Supabase enquiry database for the team to review. If you choose to continue in WhatsApp, the details shown in your message are shared with WhatsApp and PVG-EV when you send it. WhatsApp operates under its own terms and privacy policy. The website host may process technical request information when serving pages.

Browser storage

Enquiry drafts, including the details you enter and any captured GPS coordinates, are kept in session storage in the current browser tab. They are cleared after successful submission or when you choose “Start again”. Closing the tab normally ends this storage, although browser session-restoration features may restore it. The website does not save enquiry drafts in cookies or persistent local storage. Consent must be selected again after a reload. See the cookies and storage notice.

Your choices

Share only details needed for the enquiry. Avoid including identity documents, payment information or other sensitive information in free-text fields. Contact the team to request correction or deletion, or to withdraw permission for future updates. Some records may need to be retained for operational or legal obligations; ask the team about your particular record.

Local website preview

A client preview is clearly marked on each form: completing it does not send an enquiry to Supabase or WhatsApp. Session-only drafts still work while you try the form. When the site runs on localhost for testing, submitted test enquiries are stored only in the local preview log. The form displays a notice for either mode.
